# Swipe segmentation (report section 4.5) on the pilot data (evaluate_segmentation.py), run on 2026-11-24

PROVISIONAL: one person (u99: 6 swipe_left and 5 valid swipe_right clips, 26 static clips; plus the 3 u01
open-palm clips of the same hand), 2-second clips, no no_gesture recordings. This checks that the method works
on real data and gives the numbers behind the module constants; it is not a segmentation result for the report.

Reproduce with:  .venv\Scripts\python.exe evaluate_segmentation.py
                 .venv\Scripts\python.exe evaluate_segmentation.py --min-amplitude 0.3
(Python 3.10.11, numpy 2.2.6, scikit-learn 1.7.2, mediapipe 1.2.3, opencv-python 5.1.0.95: the .venv from
requirements.txt; Python 3.13.5 with numpy 2.1.3 gave the identical output)

Data used: every clip in data/landmarks (u99 and u01) without a validator ERROR, as in dataset_loader.py:
37 clips (11 swipe, 26 static); u99 swipe_right clip 5 is left out (ERROR: first stroke goes the wrong way).

Method (swipe_segmentation.py): causal, one frame at a time, on the output of live_pipeline.FrameAnalyzer
(1-euro smoothing, palm-centre speed in hand sizes per second from past frames). A cycle starts when the speed
rises above tau_on; the start position is the palm centre just before the movement. It ends in the first frame
in which the hand has gone at least swipe_min_amplitude hand sizes sideways, is back within 0.4 hand sizes of
the start (and within half of its largest excursion), and the speed is below tau_off. The label is the sign of
the first stroke in x (x increasing first = swipe_left; the stored picture is not mirrored). Rejected: slow for
0.6 s without return, onset to end shorter than 0.25 s or longer than 2 s, mostly vertical, hand lost for more
than 2 frames. Thresholds: calibration.DEFAULTS (tau_on 1.0, tau_off 0.5, swipe_min_amplitude 0.5); the run
with --min-amplitude 0.3 changes only the minimum amplitude.

Findings
1. The pause at the turning point (pilot finding, confirmed). Between the first and the last frame above
   tau_on the speed stayed below tau_off for 0 to 2 frames in the 6 swipe_left clips and in swipe_right clips
   1 and 2, but for 6, 7 and 8 frames (0.29, 0.34, 0.40 s) in swipe_right clips 3, 4 and 6 (the excluded
   clip 5 also has 6). The earlier end rule "slow for 0.2 s" (A.8, evaluate_triggers.stand_in_swipe) cuts
   exactly these 3 of the 5 valid swipe_right clips at the turning point, so their return stroke would start
   a second cycle in the opposite direction. With "back at the start" as the end condition no clip is cut.
2. Minimum amplitude 0.5 (DEFAULTS): 8 of 11 swipe clips give exactly one swipe with their own label, none
   gives a wrong label or a second swipe. Missed:
     swipe_right #4: missed: below minimum amplitude (largest excursion 0.42 hand sizes)
     swipe_right #6: missed: below minimum amplitude (largest excursion 0.35 hand sizes)
     swipe_right #3: unfinished at the end of the clip (see 4)
   The minimum amplitude is a calibrated value: per-user calibration (4.8) sets it to half of the user's
   smaller calibration swipe, within 0.25 to 1.0, so a user who swipes small can get a lower threshold.
3. Minimum amplitude 0.3: swipe_right #4 and #6 are recovered (amplitude 0.42 and 0.35, right label), 10 of 11.
   No extra swipe appears in any clip. This says little about false swipes, though: in the still clips no
   cycle starts at all (their peak speed stays below tau_on, see 5), so the minimum amplitude never comes
   into play there. Whether 0.3 lets through small incidental movements can only be measured on no_gesture
   recordings (none yet).
4. swipe_right #3 is a recording limit, not a segmentation error: the hand is back at the start (0.19 hand
   sizes) in the last frame, but its speed there is 0.51, just above tau_off 0.50; the 2-second clip ends one
   frame too early. Swipe clips are now recorded for 3 seconds (dataset_config.DURATIONS).
5. Static clips: 0 of 26 give a swipe, at both settings. Peak palm speed in the u99 still clips 0.03 to 0.18
   hand sizes per second (tau_on 1.0). The u01 open-palm clips (hand still being positioned) reach 0.17, 0.97
   and 0.60: clip 2 comes within 3 % of tau_on. Had it passed, the movement (0.58 hand sizes of drift in the
   validator) would still have to come back to its start and be at least the minimum amplitude to count.
6. Delay from movement onset to the swipe event: median 0.92 s (0.61 to 1.30 s). This is the cost of the
   out-and-back definition (A.12): the command can only come after the return stroke.
7. Margins of the constants on these clips: the cycles ended 0.02 to 0.35 hand sizes from the start (tolerance
   0.4; swipe_right #1 ended at 0.35), the vertical/horizontal extent was 0.17 to 0.86 (limit 1.0), the longest
   pause at a turning point 0.40 s (rejection after 0.6 s), the longest cycle 1.30 s (limit 2 s).
Limits: one hand, 11 swipes, 2-second clips (cycles can be cut by the clip end), no no_gesture data, so the
false-swipe rate is not measured. The 0.25 s minimum duration hardly ever acts at 20 frames per second: on
synthetic data a 0.1 s jerk already measures about 0.25 s from onset to end after smoothing
(test_segmentation.py). All constants are PROVISIONAL and must be re-checked on the training users.

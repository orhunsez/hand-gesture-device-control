# Experiment E4 (false-trigger prevention) on the pilot data (evaluate_triggers.py), run on 2026-11-23

PROVISIONAL and NOT an E4 result: one person (u99, plus the u01 open-palm clips of the same hand), no
no_gesture recordings, so the false-trigger rate per minute cannot be measured yet. The useful parts are the
latency, the repeat timing, and what happens around the swipes.

Reproduce with:  .venv\Scripts\python.exe evaluate_triggers.py               (SVM, the E1 recommendation)
                 .venv\Scripts\python.exe evaluate_triggers.py --model rules
(Python 3.13.11, numpy 2.5.5, scikit-learn 1.9.1)

Setting (TEAM_BRIEF A.10, D.4 items 10 to 13; trigger_gate.py): no activation gesture; a static gesture
presses after 3 consecutive frames (about 0.15 s) with the same label, confidence >= 0.7 and a calm hand; the
open palm alone waits 0.5 s; while held, the command repeats every 0.2 s (first repeat 0.2 s after the press);
holding tolerates 2 frames of another label and needs only confidence >= 0.5; fast movement ends a hold;
after a swipe, 1 s cooldown. Frames are played one by one through live_pipeline.FrameAnalyzer (causal: only
past frames). Static clips of u99 are analysed by a model trained without them (5 clip-level folds). The swipe
events are a STAND-IN (oracle label, placed where the palm has stayed below tau_off for 0.2 s after exceeding
tau_on), because the segmentation (4.5) and the dynamic classifier (4.7) are not written yet.

Findings
1. Start at once works: every static clip gives a press of its gesture (26 of 26). Median latency from the
   first frame with a hand: 144 to 160 ms for fist / thumb up / thumb down, 592 ms for the open palm (the
   0.5 s wait), 1153 ms for the u01 open palms (the hand was still being positioned, so it was not calm).
2. Repeats come every 0.203 s on average (setting 0.2 s; the camera gives a frame every 48 ms).
3. No hold broke up into two presses with the safeguards on ("extra" 0.00).
4. One wrong press (u01 open_palm clip 1, 0.5 s): the hand was closed for about 3 frames before it opened and
   was still, so the gate pressed thumb_down (SVM) / fist (rules). This is the price of starting after 3 frames:
   a transition pose that is held still for 0.15 s counts. A longer N_ON would stop it and add latency.
5. Open palm before a swipe (decision D.4 item 12, SVM): 3 of 11 swipe clips press the static open palm before
   the movement starts; exactly the clips whose still lead-in is longest (0.75, 0.80, 0.85 s; the others 0.40 to
   0.70 s). So with the 0.5 s wait, a palm held still for about 0.75 s or more before a swipe fired here. The rules
   do not fire here, but for another reason: in the swipe clips the thumb is tucked in, and the rules require an
   extended thumb for open_palm (they call it no_gesture), while the SVM calls it open_palm with confidence 1.0.
6. Ablation on these clips: without the confirmation the swipe clips get 1.00 / 1.36 static presses per clip
   (before / after the movement start), without the stillness check 0.36 / 0.73 (presses while the hand moves),
   all off 1.18 / 1.91. The confidence threshold, the hysteresis and the cooldown make no difference HERE:
   the SVM is confident on this one person, no hold flickered, and the swipe clips end (2 s) before the palm
   could rest after the swipe, so the cooldown is never tested. Longer clips and no_gesture data are needed.
Limits: one hand; 2-second clips; stand-in swipe events; no no_gesture clips.

# Static classifier on the pilot data (train_static.py), run on 2026-11-21

PROVISIONAL and NOT a result for the report: user u99 (one person, near, plain background, bright light,
gestures fist, open palm, thumb up, thumb down) plus the first recording tests of u01 (open palm only).
CORRECTION (same day): the user ids u01 and u99 are both the hand of the SAME person, recorded in
different sessions. Wherever this file says "other person", "second person" or "cross-user", read
"another recording session of the same hand". Nothing here says how the system treats other people.

Reproduce with:  .venv\Scripts\python.exe train_static.py     (scikit-learn 1.7.2, SVM with RBF kernel, standardised inputs)

What the run showed
1) Clip-level cross-validation inside u99 (929 stable frames, 23 clips, frames of a clip never split): 100 % for every
   class. 2) The same with smoothed or raw landmarks and with or without z: 100 % each time, so this test cannot
   choose between those options (ceiling effect). 3) Trained on all of u99, the stable open-palm frames of u01 were
   labelled open_palm 83 times and thumb_up 23 times (78 % correct).

Why the 23 frames were wrong (diagnosis, same day)
  Hand tilt = angle of the line wrist to middle-finger base from straight up in the picture.
  u01 open_palm clips (stable frames, predicted as):
    clip 1: tilt +7 deg,  24 frames -> open_palm 24
    clip 2: tilt +40 deg, 27 frames -> open_palm 24, thumb_up 3
    clip 3: tilt +68 deg,  6 frames -> thumb_up 6
    clip 4: tilt +17 deg, 15 frames -> open_palm 15
    clip 5: tilt +61 deg, 14 frames -> thumb_up 14
    clip 6: tilt +13 deg, 20 frames -> open_palm 20
  u99 mean tilt per gesture: open_palm +7 deg (clips +4..+10), fist +8 (+4..+12), thumb_up +57 (+53..+61),
  thumb_down +98 (+89..+106).
  In the training data the tilt is tied to the class: only thumb up is tilted. The model learned orientation, not
  only the finger configuration. Test: rotating the u01 frames in the picture plane to a tilt of 7 deg made
  100 % of them open_palm in every clip (as recorded: 78 %). Limits: 6 clips of one other person, and rotation in
  the picture plane only (turning the hand towards or away from the camera was not looked at).

Consequences (see TEAM_BRIEF_methodology.md, A.9 and D.5): record every gesture with different hand orientations;
add features that do not depend on rotation (distances between landmarks, joint angles) for the finger
configuration and keep the orientation only where it carries meaning (thumb up versus thumb down); or rotate
training frames randomly for the gestures where orientation does not matter.
